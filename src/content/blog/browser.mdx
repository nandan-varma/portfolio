I've always had too many browser windows open, and juggling them made it hard to actually work. So I fixed it, and built an app along the way.

## The problem, with data

After a work session, here's what I'd find open:

- One major window with all my active work.
- A couple of smaller windows I'd forgotten to close: research and documentation I only needed once.
- One-tab windows for random Google searches, ChatGPT chats, directions to weekend plans, and so on.

That doesn't sound like much, but it was all duplicated across two profiles: work and personal.

## Finding the pattern

Back in my notebook, I found a pattern. Everything fell into a 2x2: logged in vs. logged out, one-off vs. not one-off, across both profiles.

<BrowserGrid client:visible />

So I went looking for existing solutions, and found a few good ones.

## What I found

[Dia Browser](https://diabrowser.com) auto-organizes deep navigation trails into tab groups, something I'd never do manually.

I also noticed that a handful of sites — a dashboard, a chat, a quick reference — stayed open constantly just so I could glance at them, each one burning a full browser window for something that only needed a corner of the screen. So I built an app called **Peek**: it pins any URL as a small floating widget, rendered at iPhone dimensions so the mobile layout looks right, always on top of everything else, and pinned in place between sessions.

<PeekPreview client:visible />

It auto-refreshes on an interval too, so the dashboards and chats stay live without me ever switching to their tab — because most of the time, there's no tab at all.

## The final setup

Finally, I separated work and personal into two separate browsers instead of browser profiles: personal went to Brave, work went to Dia. There's a third one that doesn't fit the 2x2 at all — a plain Chrome that's never logged into anything, shared by both profiles. It's where logged-out browsing goes, and it's also what I point Claude and other browser MCPs at, so an agent poking around the web can't stumble onto an authenticated session or leak anything of mine.

Four apps now instead of one browser with a pile of windows, but I always know exactly where to look.
